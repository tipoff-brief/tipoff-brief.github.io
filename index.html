Sheet 06 / Questions

Before you start.

Where do the permits come from?

Tipoff collects public residential building-permit records from city and county open-data portals. Coverage varies by jurisdiction.

What do I get each week?

The newest matching permits for your trade and ZIP codes, with the property and job details included in the public record. The brief arrives by email.

Can I choose my area?

Yes. Report customers choose a trade and ZIP codes. Territory customers reserve an exclusive trade and ZIP combination, subject to availability.

How do I start?

Choose Report or Territory and check out. We’ll use the trade and ZIP codes you enter to prepare your first brief.

Is this a guaranteed lead list?

No. Tipoff is a research tool built from public permit filings. Records can be incomplete or delayed, and a permit does not guarantee a job or a homeowner response.

Which areas and trades do you cover?

Current data coverage: Austin, Los Angeles, Collin County, Dallas, and Sacramento. We support solar, HVAC, electrical, pool service, landscaping, fencing, insulation, remodeling, security, and window coverings. We do not sell roofing permits: a roofing permit is normally pulled by the roofer who already won the job. Territory is limited to the trades and ZIP codes we have sized; we confirm availability before activation.

Is Report exclusive?

No. Report is shared with up to 3 contractors in the same trade and ZIP code. Territory locks your trade and ZIP codes to one company, subject to availability.

How do I cancel?

Email abdul321@gmail.com with your business name. Report trials and subscriptions renew automatically until canceled; cancel before day 14 to avoid the first $297 charge. Territory is $697/month and can be canceled at the end of any billing month.

What if my area is not covered?

Email us before you check out. If we cannot deliver permit data for your trade and ZIP codes, we cancel the subscription and refund any amount charged for a period we cannot serve.
